category: breaking

- Each worker gets a seed from the job seed and its launch rank (`DISTSSHKIT_JOB_SEED`, `DISTSSHKIT_WORKER_RANK`, `DISTSSHKIT_WORKER_SEED`). The rank is the order workers were started, not `myid()`. `worker_pmap` records item-to-rank in provenance under `seeds`, schema 1. Each call keeps its own rows (`call`, `item`, `rank`), including when no worker rank was assigned. A `go` slot writes its rows into the slot snapshot at exit, and the controller merges them into `seeds.items` with `slot`. `workers` is omitted only when there are no worker rows. A `go` slot's launch rank is one of those rows when a job seed exists. `job` is omitted when there is no job seed. `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS` and `OMP_NUM_THREADS` are set only when `DISTSSHKIT_BLAS_THREADS` is set, including on a remote `go` slot after `env -u`. When that variable is unset, DistSSHRun does not assign them. A local `go` slot can still inherit them from the parent. A present `CondaPkg.toml`, `conda-lock.yml`, or `pixi.lock` is hashed into provenance. The `git` found on `PATH` is recorded under `tools` as outside the Manifest, at the start of the run with the source snapshot. Unknown provenance source keys stay ignored.
