A Julia-side auth budget applies only to the first short POSIX probe per host (cached); later sessions rely on `BatchMode=yes` and `ConnectTimeout`. `DISTRIBUTED_SSH_OPTS` cannot override `BatchMode=yes` or `RequestTTY=no`. Drive and ride run SSH preflight before worker join; zero joined workers fail closed on drive, ride, and `add_drive_workers!`. The push gate diff-lints added product lines for ad hoc `BatchMode` / raw `ssh` argv (`scripts/lint/ssh-argv.sh`, #462).
